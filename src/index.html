Common Questions

What People Ask Before They Buy.

Off-grid power decisions come with real trade-offs. Here's what we get asked most, answered straight — no upsell, no vague specs.

How big a solar panel do I need to run a mini-fridge?
✓ Quick Answer

Most 12V mini-fridges draw around 40-60W continuously. A 100W panel usually keeps one running through a full day of direct sun, but cloudy days or higher-draw fridges may call for 150-200W plus a battery buffer overnight.

What's the difference between a power station and a generator?
✓ Quick Answer

A power station stores energy in a battery and is silent and fume-free, but has a fixed capacity. A generator burns fuel to produce power continuously — it can run indefinitely with fuel, but it's louder and needs ventilation.

How long will a power station keep my devices running?
✓ Quick Answer

It depends on the station's watt-hour (Wh) capacity and what you're powering. As a rough guide, a 500Wh station runs a laptop for 8-10 hours or a small fridge for about half a day. Our power calculator can estimate your exact setup.

Can I run an RV air conditioner off a power station?
✓ Quick Answer

Only with a high-capacity station (2000Wh+) and a large enough inverter, since AC units draw a heavy startup surge. For most van life setups, a power station suits lights, fans, and electronics better than full AC.

Do I need a separate inverter if I have a power station?
✓ Quick Answer

No — nearly all power stations have a built-in inverter and standard AC outlets, so regular household devices plug in directly. A separate inverter is only needed wiring power straight from a battery bank without a station in between.
